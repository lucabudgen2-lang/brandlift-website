## What this means for our build order
1. **Reviews first-ish** — the local pack + SERP review-stars decide "website laten maken den haag". Brandlift needs to actively gather Google reviews (3 → target 25+). This is a non-code task for Luca but the highest-leverage local move.
2. **Cost guide + calculator** — low KD, incumbents are content sites (beatable with a better asset + our calculator differentiator); dewerkendewebsite is weak here.
3. **Rotterdam page** — dewerkendewebsite ranks #2 with a thin single page; a real page (like our Den Haag one) competes directly, weak field.
4. **A single `/voorbeelden` hub** — dewerkendewebsite proves one page ranks #1 for the examples cluster; the brief's plan holds.

## City SERPs — "website laten maken [stad]" (live top-10, 2026-07-13)

Delft is genuinely easy but low-reward; Utrecht/Amsterdam are for later when authority + reviews exist.
