## On-page teardown (what the winning pages actually do)
**flerque #1** (~850 words): H1 "Website laten maken Den Haag" → partner pitch → WordPress angle → 6 benefit blocks → process → **transparent pricing table on-page (€1.899 / €2.300 / op aanvraag)** → **8 named testimonials**. Personal ("Don"), heavy social proof, prices shown.

**dewerkendewebsite #2** (~950 words): H1 → client examples → process → **a big generic "Bedrijven in Den Haag" economy section** (government, ICT, tourism…) with authoritative outbound links (Vredespaleis, Wikipedia, Universiteit Leiden) = deliberate **local-topical-relevance content** → Webdesign/SEO Den Haag → CTA.

**Tactics that win here that we don't yet do:**
1. **Transparent pricing indication on-page** (flerque shows €1.899–2.300). We say only "gratis groeigesprek." Worth adding a price-from indication for trust + qualification.
2. **On-page testimonials with names** (flerque: 8). We have the Reviews block but only 3.
3. **Local-relevance content** (dewerkendewebsite's Den Haag economy block + outbound authority links). Our page has wijken; could add richer local context.
4. Our edge: Next.js static = faster than their WordPress (minor, but real CWV win).

## The honest conclusion for Brandlift
- **On-page alone will NOT crack the top 3 for "website laten maken den haag."** flerque (96 page links + 285 domains) and dewerkendewebsite (727 domains) are defended by authority we can't match short-term. Our page is structurally as good or better — but links win here.
- **Highest-leverage SEO actions, in order:**
  1. **Reviews** (Track 2) — decides the local pack, on every city.
  2. **Footer-credit-link flywheel** — put "Website door Brandlift" → brandliftagency.nl in the footer of EVERY client site (Eykelenboom, RotorSwing, De Reizende Kwast, and all future builds). This is literally how every competitor here built authority. Free, compounding, and the #1 way to close the domain-authority gap.
  3. **Target the low-authority-barrier keywords first** — the **cost cluster** (KD 3–4, content-led SERP, NOT gated by local pack or 300-domain incumbents; dewerkendewebsite's own cost page only ranks #22–27) and **low-competition cities** (Delft). These can rank on great on-page + a handful of links, without needing 300 referring domains.
- **On-page upgrades to make now:** add a price-from indication, surface more testimonials as we gather reviews, and enrich local context on city pages.
