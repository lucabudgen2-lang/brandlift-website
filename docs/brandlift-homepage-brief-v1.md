## Page Purpose

The homepage should clearly position Brandlift as a strategic website partner for Dutch companies that want more visibility, more trust and more requests.

It should not try to rank as the main page for `website laten maken`. That job belongs to `/diensten/website-laten-maken`. The homepage should act as the central brand and conversion hub, linking users into the right service, audience, case and contact pages.

## Strategic Role

The homepage must answer four questions quickly:

1. What does Brandlift do?
2. Who is it for?
3. Why is Brandlift different?
4. What should I do next?

The answer:

Brandlift builds strategic websites with branding, local SEO and conversion for Dutch companies that want more visibility, more trust and more requests.

## Search Intent

Mixed intent:

- Brand search
- Commercial investigation
- Referral traffic validation
- Founder/trust validation
- Local validation for Den Haag

The homepage should not be over-optimized for one generic keyword. It should support the whole SEO structure.

Keep Den Haag present, but do not make it the main homepage angle.

Use Den Haag in:

- A short trust/location line in the hero or intro
- The footer
- The contact section
- The About/founder section
- Internal link to future `/website-laten-maken-den-haag`

Example wording:

`Gevestigd in Den Haag, actief voor bedrijven in heel Nederland.`

Avoid making the homepage feel like it only serves Den Haag.

## Recommended Page Structure

### 1. Hero

Goal:

Immediately communicate what Brandlift does and why it matters.

Recommended content:

- H1: `Strategische websites voor meer zichtbaarheid, vertrouwen en aanvragen`
- Supporting copy: Brandlift builds websites with branding, local SEO and conversion for Dutch companies.
- Primary CTA: `Plan een gratis groeigesprek`
- Secondary CTA: `Bekijk onze werkwijze` or `Bekijk cases`
- Small location/trust line: `Gevestigd in Den Haag, actief in heel Nederland.`

Notes:

Keep this sharp. The hero should not list every service in detail.

### 2. Problem / Recognition Section

Goal:

Make the right visitor feel understood.

Recommended angle:

Many companies have a website, but it does not clearly explain their value, rank locally, or turn visitors into requests.

Possible section heading:

`Een mooie website is niet genoeg als klanten je niet vinden of vertrouwen`

Points to cover:

- The website looks fine but generates too few requests.
- Services are unclear.
- Local visibility is weak.
- The brand does not feel as professional as the work behind it.
- Visitors do not know why they should choose the company.

### 3. Brandlift Method / Difference

Goal:

Explain why Brandlift is different from generic webdesign, cheap website builders and broad marketing agencies.

Possible section heading:

`De Brandlift aanpak: strategie, zichtbaarheid en conversie in één website`

Core pillars:

- Strategy: clarify offer, audience and positioning.
- Branding: create a professional and trustworthy impression.
- Website structure: build pages around services, proof and decision-making.
- Local SEO: help the right people find the business.
- Conversion: guide visitors toward contact.

## Homepage Do's

- Lead with the full Brandlift promise.
- Mention Den Haag naturally, not aggressively.
- Use clear links to the actual SEO landing pages.
- Show cases as strategic proof.
- Make the CTA obvious and repeated.
- Keep copy buyer-friendly, not SEO-jargon-heavy.

## Homepage Don'ts

- Do not make the homepage target only `website laten maken Den Haag`.
- Do not stuff every keyword into the hero.
- Do not position Brandlift as a generic full-service marketing agency.
- Do not lead with cheap/snel website messaging.
- Do not make branding feel like a separate disconnected service.

## Recommended First Draft Direction

The homepage should feel like:

`Brandlift helps good businesses become easier to find, easier to trust and easier to contact.`

The main story:

1. Your website should do more than look good.
2. Brandlift combines strategy, branding, local SEO and conversion.
3. This is especially useful for service businesses and vakbedrijven.
4. Brandlift is based in Den Haag and works across the Netherlands.
5. Start with a free growth conversation.
